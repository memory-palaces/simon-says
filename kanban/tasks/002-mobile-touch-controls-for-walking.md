Pointer Lock is mouse-only, so walk mode cannot engage on phones at all. Needs a touch controller: drag-to-look, virtual joystick or tap-to-move, on-screen button for T (drop locus). Bird's-eye (V) + tap-to-travel is probably the better primary navigation on touch.
