Parked deliberately. Text-only sharing was judged unsatisfying: people iterate on images/3D and the pixels are much of the value. Hosting other people's generated images invites copyright/abuse liability on the host's domain. Open questions: is a 'share copy (text only)' export worth shipping at all, or is a weak version worse than none? Does GLB-by-URL plus a palace-JSON-by-URL loader get close enough without hosting anything? Revisit once people actually want to publish.
